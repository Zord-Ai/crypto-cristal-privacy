Privacy Policy

Crypto Cristal is a personal automated tool that generates and publishes crypto news content to TikTok. We do not collect, store, or share any user data with third parties. The app only accesses the TikTok account @crypto.cristal to publish video content via the TikTok Content Posting API.

The only data stored is an OAuth access token saved locally on the operator's machine, used solely to authenticate API requests.

Terms of Service

This app is for personal use by the owner of the @crypto.cristal TikTok account. All published content is original, generated from publicly available crypto news sources. The app complies with TikTok's Developer Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
